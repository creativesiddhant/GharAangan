Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

At Ghar Aangan, we strive to offer you pure, handcrafted Himalayan Pahadi products of the highest quality. We understand that plans change, and we want to make our cancellation and refund process as transparent and stress-free as possible.

1. Booking & Pre-Dispatch Cancellations

For orders and reservations placed through our website booking portal:

  • Zero Risk Booking: If you placed an order reservation without upfront payment, you can cancel your reservation at any time before dispatch with no questions asked.
  • How to Cancel: Simply inform our team via WhatsApp/SMS or Phone Call at +91 7292002260, or email us at manisha.kailash14@gmail.com to cancel your booking.
  • If we are unable to reach you to confirm your shipping address within 5 days of placing the booking, the reservation may be automatically canceled to free up stock for other customers.

2. Order Cancellations (After Payment, Before Dispatch)

If you have completed your payment online and wish to cancel before the shipment has been dispatched, you are eligible for a 100% full refund:

  • To cancel a paid order, notify us promptly via WhatsApp or phone call at +91 7292002260 or email us at manisha.kailash14@gmail.com.
  • Once processed, the entire order amount will be refunded back to your original payment method (bank account, UPI, credit/debit card) within 5 to 7 business days.

3. Returns Policy (After Delivery)

Due to the consumable nature of our Pahadi food items and food safety regulations, we do not accept returns once the product package has been delivered and opened. This helps us guarantee the absolute purity, authenticity, and hygiene of our small-batch foods for all customers.

4. Damaged or Leaking Shipments

Your satisfaction and safety are our top priorities. If you receive a shipment that has been damaged in transit, shows signs of leakage, or has broken seals/glass:

  • Please inspect the package at the time of delivery.
  • Take clear photographs or a short video showing the damaged packaging and product labels.
  • Contact us via WhatsApp at +91 7292002260 or email us at manisha.kailash14@gmail.com within 7 days of delivery with the invoice/order details and media files.
  • Upon verifying transit damage, we will arrange for a free replacement to be shipped immediately, or process a 100% full refund, based on your preference.

5. Refund Settlement Timelines

All approved refunds are initiated immediately from our side. It typically takes 5 to 7 business days for the refunded amount to reflect in your bank account or payment source, depending on your card issuer or banking partner's settlement cycle.

6. Contact Us

If you have any further questions regarding cancellations, damaged items, or refund status, feel free to get in touch with our helpdesk:

Email: manisha.kailash14@gmail.com
Phone/WhatsApp: +91 7292002260 (Mon-Sat, 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM)
